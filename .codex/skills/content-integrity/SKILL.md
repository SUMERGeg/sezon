---
name: content-integrity
description: Keep the SEZON portfolio concept commercially believable without inventing real business claims, reviews, scarcity, delivery promises, company history, customer metrics, or actual order/payment behavior.
---

Use when adding or reviewing copy, product data, checkout, trust blocks, SEO metadata, structured data, and portfolio case content.

## Rules

Allowed:
- demonstration prices/stock clearly within concept context;
- realistic product descriptions;
- proposed delivery examples;
- conceptual process imagery with appropriate labeling.

Disallowed unless verified and intentionally converting to a real business:
- customer reviews;
- awards;
- founded date/team biographies;
- real-sounding address/contact identity;
- order numbers;
- claims such as “5000 customers”, “same-day guaranteed”, “best florist”;
- fake discount anchors/scarcity timers;
- payment collection.

Check that checkout visibly states it is a demonstration and final CTA says `Завершить демонстрацию`.
