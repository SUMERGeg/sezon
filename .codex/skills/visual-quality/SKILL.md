Use for visual audits, page polish, responsive art direction, typography, spacing, imagery, component states, and motion.

## Visual north star

A warm editorial flower brand with a disciplined commerce layer.

Protect:
- milk/paper background;
- deep botanical green structure;
- restrained petal/wine accents;
- serif display + neutral sans UI;
- large floral photography;
- editorial home + regular commerce pages;
- subtle motion.

Reject:
- generic SaaS cards;
- glassmorphism;
- gradients everywhere;
- excessive pills;
- giant empty sections with little meaning;
- cursors/particles/petals floating continuously;
- UI text over busy photos without contrast.

## Audit

Check in order:
1. hierarchy;
2. composition/rhythm;
3. typography;
4. image crop/quality;
5. spacing consistency;
6. states and affordances;
7. mobile recomposition;
8. motion restraint.

Every suggested visual change must improve hierarchy, comprehension, trust, or product desirability.
